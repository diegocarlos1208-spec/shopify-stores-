Description

The Spicy Lip Plumper uses a warm botanical tingle complex to gently boost micro-circulation, giving lips an instant, natural-looking flush of volume and colour. Underneath the gloss, a tri-peptide blend plus hyaluronic acid work over time to smooth vertical lip lines, soften lipstick bleed and leave lips cushiony rather than dry. No needles, no lidocaine, no harsh sting.

Key Ingredients
  • Spicy Tingle Complex (capsicum, ginger root, wintergreen) — the warmth that draws blood flow for instant plump
  • Tri-Peptide Blend — supports natural collagen for smoother texture with daily use
  • Hyaluronic Acid + Vitamin E — pulls in moisture and holds it, so the plump feels cushioned
  • Vegan · cruelty-free · no parabens · dermatologist-tested
How to Use

Sweep one thin layer over clean lips. Expect a warm tingle within 60 seconds and visible plump in about 5 minutes. Wear alone as a gloss, as a base under lip liner and lipstick, or dab over the top for extra shine. Reapply through the day as needed. Patch-test before first use.
